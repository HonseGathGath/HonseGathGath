I like understanding systems from the metal up — how a chip actually executes an
instruction, why one design is faster than another, what the OS is really doing
underneath the abstraction. Most of what I build lives somewhere between hardware
and software: FPGAs, low-level C, and whatever breaks when you try to make
something faster than it has any right to be.

> I'd rather understand why something works than just that it does.
